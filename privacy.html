Privacy Policy

cecco

Overview

cecco does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device and works offline.

cecco computes everything it shows — the planetary day and hour, the moon’s phase, sign, and mansion, and the content it suggests — locally on your device from the date, time, and location you provide. None of this leaves your device.

We do not use analytics or advertising, we do not track you, and we do not create user accounts.

If you grant cecco access to your device location, it is used only on your device to compute your local astrological reading and is never transmitted or stored off-device.

We do not sell your data or share it with data brokers, and we never use anything we collect to profile or track you.

Crash diagnostics

To find and fix bugs, cecco uses Sentry to collect anonymous crash and error reports. These are strictly diagnostic and not linked to your identity: they contain no account, no name, no device location, and nothing that ties a report to you. They are never used to track you or for advertising.
